Whether you are learning a codebase or a broader topic, generated content never becomes a scheduled card by accident. Every prompt passes through an inspectable workflow.

    Explore a system or topic

    Work through code, documentation, research, or study material with your agent.

    Generate recall prompts

    The agent proposes concise drafts grounded in the material, with rationale and sources.

  3. 03

    Try and approve

    Answer it, edit it, and accept only what belongs in your study plan.

  4. 04

    Study anywhere

    FSRS-7 schedules each review identically across the desktop app, terminal, and iPhone.

Local by default

Your decks, cards, assets, and complete review history live on your device in SQLite.

FSRS-7 in Rust

Modern scheduling with configurable retention, learning steps, daily limits, and weight optimization.

Agent-first CLI

Create source-grounded drafts from codebases and research with Codex, Claude Code, pi, or your own scripts.

Native iOS companion

SwiftUI iPhone app backed by the same Rust core via UniFFI — offline review, Quick Capture with Photos, stats, and daily reminders.

Optional Cloud sync

Sync decks, reviews, and media across desktop, CLI, and iPhone via rem Cloud or your own self-hosted Cloudflare Worker.

Rich Markdown & backups

Syntax-highlighted code, images, GIFs, tags, and full-fidelity portable JSON backups.
